Answers before the call

Do you work with companies at the incorporation stage?

Yes. We regularly support founders setting up their first Luxembourg entity, whether a SARL, SA or SOPARFI holding structure. We coordinate with a notary and help you choose the right vehicle for your activity.

What accounting software do you work with?

We are well-versed with different accounting software. If you're starting fresh, we'll recommend the right fit for your size and reporting needs.

How does fixed-fee pricing actually work?

After our discovery call, we send a proposal with a clearly scoped annual fee. Everything in the scope is included — no hourly rates, no add-on charges for standard requests. If your needs change materially, we discuss and adjust together.

What's your typical response time?

We commit to a sub-24-hour response on all standard requests during business days. For urgent compliance matters — a deadline, an investor request, a regulator query — we prioritise same-day turnaround.
